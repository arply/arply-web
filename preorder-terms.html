Preorder terms

What the €1 reservation deposit is, your right to withdraw from it, how refunds work, and the disclaimer that comes with a DIY, non-certified piece of hardware.

Before you rely on this page: it's a structural starting point based on the standard German/EU statutory withdrawal-notice template, not legal advice tailored to your situation. Have it checked — a lawyer, or a paid generator like e‑recht24 or IT‑Recht Kanzlei — before accepting real payments. Also worth checking before you actually ship assembled electronics into Germany: ElektroG registration with stiftung ear and, if you ship packaging, VerpackG registration via LUCID. Neither is covered below.

1. What the €1 covers

The €1 payment is a reservation deposit, not the purchase price of a finished unit. It reserves you a place in the build/shipping queue and is deducted from the final price when a ready-made arply ships. Paying it does not commit you to buying the finished unit — you can withdraw as described below, and if arply never ships, the deposit is refunded in full.

2. Right of withdrawal

Because this is a distance contract with a consumer, German and EU law give you a 14-day right of withdrawal from the deposit. The statutory notice below is the legally prescribed wording (Muster-Widerrufsbelehrung); the English version is a translation for convenience.

English

Right of withdrawal

You have the right to withdraw from this contract within 14 days without giving any reason.

The withdrawal period is 14 days from the day the contract was concluded (i.e. the day you pay the deposit).

To exercise your right of withdrawal, you must inform us — Till Scheffler, Martin-Luther-Platz 11, 01099 Dresden, Germany, info@arply.de — of your decision to withdraw from this contract by a clear statement (e.g. a letter sent by post or an email). You may use the model withdrawal form below, but it is not obligatory.

To meet the withdrawal deadline, it is sufficient for you to send your notice of withdrawal before the withdrawal period has expired.

Effects of withdrawal

If you withdraw from this contract, we will reimburse all payments received from you without undue delay, and at the latest within 14 days from the day we receive notice of your withdrawal. We will use the same means of payment you used for the original transaction, unless expressly agreed otherwise; in no event will you be charged any fees as a result of the reimbursement.

4. Refunds outside the withdrawal window

  • If arply development is discontinued or a reserved unit can't be delivered for any reason, deposits are refunded in full, no withdrawal request needed.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method through Ko‑fi. Processing time follows Ko‑fi's own payout timelines — expect roughly 7–10 business days, though this isn't guaranteed by us.
  • To request a refund, contact info@arply.de with your order details.
